# Unofficial Marvel Rivals Ignite: Teams Power Rankings

## Information
* The rating is calculated only from a team’s own match and tournament results.
  * Pacific Asia and Pacific Oceania inculed only tournament result, points for matches are awarded only in Pacific Playoffs.
* Higher-tier events (international LANs) have a greater impact than regional stages. Knockout matches matter more than group-stage matches.
* A win increases the rating. A loss decreases it. The size of the change depends on the importance of the match and the other factors below.
* Beating a stronger team produces a larger gain. Losing to a weaker team produces a larger loss. Results against teams of similar strength have a moderate effect.
* Dominant series wins (clear sweeps) give a bigger boost. Close series wins give a smaller boost. Dominant losses hurt more than close losses.
